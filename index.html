Mid-Week Swindle

Podium finish, points, prizes and next-playing handicap

Swindle Rules

  1. Highest score today: HCP deducted by 1.0 shot
  2. 2nd highest score today: HCP deducted by 0.5 shot
  3. Other players do not lose shots unless another rule applies
  4. Exceptional Score 40 and over - loses an additional 2 shots
  5. 31 and lower scores gain 0.5 shot on HCP, unless the player is the highest or 2nd highest score
  6. Maximum PHC is 18
  7. Payouts are based on PGA Matrix
  8. £5 to be paid before start of round or Cash dependant on group choice
  9. Scorecard to be completed on course and no adjustments in clubhouse accepted, unless damaged by weather
